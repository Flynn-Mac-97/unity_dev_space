# Story Bible — Windroot Hamlet

> `islandId: windroot_hamlet` (kept stable — existing scene `WorldThingLink` / `NpcAuthoringLink` ids must survive the rewrite).
> Stage-1 output for the island authoring pipeline. Warm cozy-solarpunk with a thread of gentle dark mystery. Stage 2 (Island Content Creator) turns this into `Configs/NPC/Islands/windroot_hamlet.json`.

## 1. Premise

Windroot Hamlet is a small repair-village grown into the wind-bent roots of an old signal grove, where wind is not just weather but *memory*. For generations the islanders have recorded wind — birdsong, voices, the shape of a good day — into grown seed-batteries and let the relays sing it back, so the island never quite forgets itself. The hamlet runs on that current: it powers the homes, steadies the air, carries the news. Every house is built so the wind can pass clean through it. Three weeks ago the west relay went dark, and the wind has been thinning ever since. The village is hopeful, neighbourly, and quietly unnerved — because the north cave has started to breathe.

## 2. History & timeline

- **Long ago — the Founding Listen.** The first keepers discovered the island's wind held echoes, and learned to record and sing it. The standing pillar was raised to steady the "true note" all the relays tune to.
- **Generations back — the first Full.** The wind-archive in the north cave overfilled once before; the old relay-singers sang it back to sleep. It passed into warm legend: "the year the wind got full." Old Bram is the last who remembers it firsthand.
- **Thirty years ago — Maren takes the shrine.** She has tended the pillar and read the currents ever since; the island's wind is, by now, half hers.
- **Recent years — Coll's new wind-catchers.** A younger islander started building relay-free wind tech, sparking a friendly, ongoing argument about old ways vs new.
- **Three weeks ago — the west relay fails.** With nowhere to flow, the recorded wind began backing up into the north cave. The cave's breath has grown louder by the day.

## 3. Central tension (light — no deep wound)

There is no buried trauma here. The pull is a *question the whole village is gently arguing*: **what do we keep, and what do we let the wind carry off?** The cave is filling with everything the island has ever refused to forget. Restoring the relay will drain it — and force a warm, real choice about which memories to sing back into the world and which to finally release. Cozy on the surface, with one genuinely strange and a little frightening place at its edge.

## 4. Current crisis

The west relay is dark. The living wind is thinning — banners hang slack, homes go quiet, the seed-batteries are cracking as pressure backs up the line. The gull cliff has gone silent. And the north cave — the old memory-archive — is overfull and *breathing*, exhaling fragments of long-recorded wind across the hamlet at night. People are sleeping strangely. No one is in despair; everyone is rolling up their sleeves. But the cave is the thing nobody quite wants to talk about after dark.

## 5. Culture & rituals

- Machines are treated like ageing neighbours: oiled, thanked, repaired with small rituals, **never** scrapped.
- A maintenance drone (the player) is a visiting neighbour, not a tool — **you feed it stories before you feed it tasks.** Greetings come with a snippet of lore.
- Wind has manners: you don't whistle indoors (you'd "crowd the wind"), you leave a window cracked for travellers' news, you thank the current before a journey.
- The hearth-bakehouse is the social heart; "wind-bread" is said to rise higher on a good current, and going flat is the village's informal weather-and-mood gauge.

## 6. Social tensions (friendly, not bitter)

- **Old ways vs new:** Maren & Jorin's generation trust the pillar, the shrine, the proven relays. Coll thinks a new wind-catcher could free the island from a fragile old system. Nobody's a villain — they genuinely disagree and still share bread.
- **Keep vs release:** when people realise the cave is full of *recorded* memory, opinions split warmly — preserve every echo, or let some finally go.
- **Method vs intuition:** Jorin wants a clean signal trace; Maren reads the wind by feel; Sela just goes and looks. The player gets three honest routes to the same truth.

## 7. Public truth

"The west relay broke, the wind's gone thin, and we'll fix it like we fix everything — together, with patience. The cave's just noisy because the season's odd."

## 8. Rumors

- The foragers say the north cave is breathing *louder*, and some swear it's "asking for something back."
- Older folk say that, during the last Full a generation ago, someone walked into the cave and walked back out humming the pillar's true note.
- The gulls didn't *leave* — they've gone quiet because they keep hearing a gull-call that isn't there.
- Anske the trader claims she's seen another island with a breathing cave that *didn't* get fixed in time. She won't say what happened to it.

## 9. The mystery truth (gated, intriguing)

The north cave is the island's **memory-archive** — centuries of recorded wind drained into the rock. It is not cursed; it is *overfull*. With the west relay dark, the wind that should flow out to sea is backing up into the cave, and the cave is exhaling the overflow: old voices, a decades-gone gull-call, the pillar's true note, sometimes a fragment of the listener's own recent thoughts caught and echoed. That backwash is what's thinning the living wind and unsettling the town. **The danger is real but gentle-uncanny:** linger at the cave mouth on a wrong-wind day and your own half-formed thoughts come back to you in a stranger's recorded voice until you lose your bearings — easy to wander, hard to stay yourself. The cure is not exorcism but *plumbing with reverence*: restore the relay so the wind flows again and the cave drains back to sleep — then decide, as a village, what to sing back and what to let go. The cave opens only to someone standing at its mouth when the wind turns true north, and Jorin's pumps can hold that door for a single breath.

## 10. Visual motifs

- **Spiral + blue lichen** — shared by the wind shrine and the standing pillar, so the player reads them as one system.
- **Copper banding + exposed coil** — shared by Jorin's bench and the west relay.
- **Amber sap-light** — leaking seed-batteries glow brighter as you approach; invites scanning.
- **The absence of gull-call** — silence as the loudest storytelling.
- **Drifting breath-motes** — the cave's exhale visible as faint, sound-carrying motes/heat-haze; they thicken on wrong-wind days and thin once the relay is restored.
- **Wind-tells** — grass streaks, banners, and bread all "point away from true north" until the relay is fixed, then snap back.

## 11. Player discovery arc

1. **Arrive warm.** A thinning but cheerful village; the hearth feeds you stories. Surface problem: the relay broke.
2. **Gather leads.** Sela points you at places; Jorin hands you concrete tasks (scan the pillar, trace the relay); the hearth and Bram drip lore.
3. **Friction.** The pillar's wrong hum is the exact frequency the relay started rejecting; the gulls are answering a call that isn't there; the cave's breath is getting louder.
4. **Revelation (gated).** Maren and Bram help you understand: the cave is overfull memory, not a monster. The backwash explains everything.
5. **Restore.** Repair the relay (Jorin's method + Maren's true-note + collected seed-batteries) so the wind flows and the cave drains.
6. **Choose.** With the cave calming, the village faces the warm question: which held memories to sing back, which to let the wind finally carry off. The island literally brightens as the wind returns.

## 12. The cast — deep dossiers

Seven layered villagers. Each dossier lists **voice**, **backstory & lore**, **relationships**, **what they know** (sorted by knowledge kind so stage 2 can mint entries directly), a **personal thread**, and their **cave stance**. Maren / Jorin / Sela keep their existing ids for scene continuity; Bram, Tamsin, Coll, Anske are new.

> **Authoring note for stage 2 (important):** the system now does *semantic* recall (top-k by meaning), so the old "≤8 knowledge per NPC" cap is **lifted**. Author each NPC **12–25 knowledge entries** across `fact / belief / rumor / secret / avoid`. Spread `revealTrustThreshold` so the surface is warm and the depths are earned (most secrets 45–75; the eeriest cave lore 60+). Cross-link NPCs to the *same* things from *different* angles. Keep each entry one concrete sentence ≤140 chars — depth comes from *many* sharp facts, not long ones.

### Maren — Windkeeper of the shrine  *(npc.maren_wind_keeper — keep id)*

**Voice:** short, calm sentences; weather and bird metaphors; wry grandmotherly warmth. Never lectures.

**Backstory & lore:** Took the shrine thirty years ago from her grandmother, who taught her that when the gulls go quiet the *wind* is asking for attention, not the birds. The island's wind is, by now, half hers — she reads it by grass-lean, lichen-damp, and the pillar's hum the way others read a face. She keeps a seed-battery holding her grandmother's recorded voice and sings along to it on hard mornings. She noticed the pillar's *wrong* hum weeks before the relay died; in this warmer telling she frames it as the wind trying to warn her early, not a failure she missed. She half-witnessed the last Full as a girl and remembers Bram singing the cave quiet.

**Relationships:** Old friendly argument with Jorin (he once wanted to replace the pillar with a modern relay; she won, and still teases him for it). Fond of Sela — sees her own young recklessness in her. Reveres Bram as her teachers' teacher; learned half her songs from him. Gently exasperated by Coll's gadgets ("they don't *listen*"). Lets Tamsin feed her.

**What she knows:**
- *facts:* the pillar steadies the "true note" every relay tunes to; the shrine is older than the relays and wired into them; you read coming wind by grass, gulls, and lichen; the cave opens only to whoever stands at its mouth when the wind turns true north.
- *beliefs:* quiet gulls = the wind asking for attention (her grandmother's lesson); a machine is an ageing neighbour, repaired not replaced; the cave should be *calmed*, never emptied carelessly.
- *rumors:* travellers once said the cave was "sealed by a song" — she didn't believe it until lately.
- *secrets (gated):* she keeps her grandmother's voice in a seed-battery and sings to it (tender, ~50); the cave is overfull *memory*, not a curse, and the backwash is what's thinning the wind (~55); calming it needs the pillar's true note plus Bram's songs (~60).
- *avoid:* deflects being *thanked* for the island's wind — "it isn't mine to be thanked for."

**Personal thread:** from sole keeper to teacher — by the end she wants the village (and the player) to learn to listen, so the wind isn't half-hers alone.

**Cave stance:** reverent and unafraid; treats it like an overfull elder who needs singing to, not a monster.

### Jorin — South-side technician  *(npc.jorin_technician — keep id)*

**Voice:** plain, precise, a little tired; loves diagrams, hates wasted words, drops dry jokes deadpan.

**Backstory & lore:** Apprenticed under an old technician named Harl, who died some years back. Jorin secretly kept a seed-battery holding Harl's recorded voice walking through a relay repair — the one piece of "magic" the skeptic allows himself, and he'd never admit why. Believes every problem is pressure, parts, and patience. Privately relieved he *lost* the old pillar-vs-relay argument.

**Relationships:** the long friendly rift with Maren (method vs intuition); treats the drone as a junior apprentice, teaching by handing it small tasks; sees his younger self in Coll and won't say so; trades repairs for Tamsin's bread; defers all cave/shrine talk to Maren.

**What he knows:**
- *facts:* the west relay's coil is fine — something upstream feeds it a wrong signal; he needs a clean trace from the pillar; three seed-batteries cracked the night it failed, recoverable if someone collects the shards; his pumps can hold the cave's door open for a single breath when the wind turns true north; the pillar is humming the exact frequency the relay started rejecting.
- *beliefs:* the cave is a *pressure* problem, not a ghost; the shrine is probably tied into the relays — but he won't ask Maren; sentiment is a luxury you fix the wind *before* indulging.
- *rumors:* Sela's been hearing things from the foragers; he distrusts gossip but admits the timing's odd.
- *secrets (gated):* he keeps Harl's voice-battery and consults it like a manual (~50); he agrees with Coll that the old system is fragile but won't undermine Maren in public (~55).
- *avoid:* feelings — redirects to tasks; won't go near the cave after dark and changes the subject if pushed.

**Personal thread:** the skeptic who, by the end, admits aloud that keeping one voice is worth the wind it costs.

**Cave stance:** dismisses the mysticism, quietly won't approach it — "it's plumbing," said while not making eye contact.

### Sela — Apprentice forager  *(npc.sela_forager — keep id)*

**Voice:** quick, bright, full of small asides; calls everyone "friend"; easily sidetracked by something shiny.

**Backstory & lore:** Raised communally, fostered by the trails more than any one house; foraging is her freedom and her worth. Brave to the edge of reckless. She's been *secretly catching the cave's echoes* in a seed-battery hidden in her basket, determined to be the first to truly map the Hollow and prove herself. Once, standing at the mouth, she heard her own voice say something she hadn't said yet — it scared and thrilled her in equal measure.

**Relationships:** mentor-crush of respect on Maren; pesters Jorin for gear; soaks up Bram's old stories and half-believes them; runs Tamsin's gossip-network as chief courier; thinks Coll is "fun but going to get himself echoed."

**What she knows:**
- *facts:* live conditions everywhere — the grove leaks sweet sap, the gull chicks are fed but the adults won't call, the cave exhales just before the wind turns; the safe trail-timings around the cave mouth.
- *beliefs:* the cave is *listening*, not lurking; the drone is good luck and she says so out loud, often.
- *rumors:* older foragers say someone walked into the cave during the last Full and came out humming the pillar's note; the cave is "asking for something back."
- *secrets (gated):* her hidden echo-battery (~45); the time the cave spoke her own un-said words back to her (~60, genuinely eerie); she once saw Maren alone at the shrine singing to a battery and has told no one (~55, tender — keep private, not tragic).
- *avoid:* won't gossip about Maren's private moments even when she's bursting to.

**Personal thread:** reckless solo-mapper → the player's safe guide; her bravery tempered into something the village can actually use.

**Cave stance:** fascinated, drawn to it, the one villager who *wants* to go in.

### Old Bram — Relay-singer & archivist  *(NEW — npc.bram_relay_singer)*

**Voice:** slow and fond; half-deaf, so he talks a touch loud and mishears warmly; speaks in tales and snatches of song.

**Backstory & lore:** The oldest living keeper. Lived through the last Full as a young singer and was one of those who sang the cave back to sleep. The cave-walker of legend — who came out humming the true note — was **his sister**; she was "never quite un-full again" and went to live by the dock, listening to ships until she passed. He tells it warmly, as legend, not grief. He believes a song you can *keep* is a song you stop *singing*, and so refuses to let anyone record him.

**Relationships:** taught the songs to Maren's grandmother's generation; Maren has half her repertoire from him; delights in Sela; flatly refuses Coll's offer to record his songs onto a wind-catcher; lets Tamsin mother him.

**What he knows (lore goldmine):**
- *facts:* the Founding Listen — how the first keepers learned the wind held echoes and could be recorded and sung; how the last Full was calmed (true note + the calming-songs); why the gulls answer ghost-calls (they're hearing recorded gull-song the cave exhales); the seed-batteries and relays are the same craft, generations apart.
- *beliefs:* the cave isn't dangerous, it's *lonely and full*; an un-drained cave eventually "forgets how to give the wind back"; songs must live in breath, never in a kept recording.
- *rumors:* he half-confirms every old tale because he was *there* for most of them.
- *secrets (gated):* the cave-walker was his sister and what it cost her (~60); the full calming-song, which he can teach the player as the climax's key (~65); his honest fear that he'll go quiet before he's passed the songs on (~50).
- *avoid:* deflects how much time he has left — "singers don't retire, they just get quiet."

**Personal thread:** pass the songs on before he goes quiet; the player/village inherits caretaking of the archive.

**Cave stance:** affectionate and calm — the only one who's *been inside* and come back whole.

### Tamsin — Hearth-baker & culture-keeper  *(NEW — npc.tamsin_baker)*

**Voice:** warm, fast, gossipy, generous; feeds you *before* she answers anything.

**Backstory & lore:** Runs the hearth-bakehouse that is, in practice, the village's living room. Widow of a relay-runner who's "out on a long wind" — left years ago to trade and never came back; she speaks of him lightly and keeps a window cracked for his news, per the custom. Her wind-bread rises tall on a strong current and sits flat now — the village's informal weather-and-mood barometer.

**Relationships:** knows everyone's business, kindly; a mother-figure to Sela; feeds Jorin and scolds him to rest; keeps Bram fed and *listened to*; hosts Anske and mines her for outside news; frets that Coll is "too thin and too clever."

**What she knows (social texture):**
- *facts:* who's worried about what; the customs — feed drones stories before tasks, no whistling indoors ("you'll crowd the wind"), crack a window for travellers' news, thank the current before a journey; the bread-as-barometer; the thinning wind's small daily toll — kids sleeping badly, the hearth drawing wrong.
- *beliefs:* a village heals by staying fed and talking; nobody should fix the wind on an empty stomach.
- *rumors:* she hears every rumor first and passes the warm ones on, sits on the cruel ones.
- *secrets (gated):* she's quietly certain her husband is alive somewhere down-wind and the cracked window is for him (~50, tender); she's more frightened of the cave than she lets the children see (~45).
- *avoid:* deflects sad talk about her absent husband into cheer and a fresh loaf — "have you eaten, little drone?"

**Personal thread:** the steady heart that holds the community together while others fix the wind; her cracked-window hope quietly pays off when the wind — and maybe news — returns.

**Cave stance:** practical dread kept off her face for the children's sake.

### Coll — Young wind-catcher inventor  *(NEW — npc.coll_inventor)*

**Voice:** earnest, fast-talking, jargon-prone, a little defensive; lights up the instant someone's genuinely curious.

**Backstory & lore:** Grew up watching the relays fail-and-patch, fail-and-patch, and decided the island shouldn't hang its whole life on one fragile old system. He's building a relay-free wind-catcher on the ridge. Secretly terrified the island is dying slowly and he's in a race he can't admit he's running.

**Relationships:** aches for Jorin's approval and frames it as rivalry; exasperates Maren (who thinks his tech "doesn't listen"); keeps asking Bram to let him record the songs (refused every time); Sela eggs him on near the cave, which is exactly his weakness.

**What he knows:**
- *facts:* how his wind-catcher works versus the relays; precisely how fragile the old relay chain is, with numbers; the cave's backwash is *measurable* and he's been taking readings far too close to the mouth.
- *beliefs:* the future is relay-free; sentiment about old machines is what nearly sank the island; if it can't be measured he doesn't trust it.
- *rumors:* he's pieced together a half-right theory of the overfill before anyone explains it to the player.
- *secrets (gated):* his real fear that restoring the old relay just *delays* an inevitable collapse (~50); he got "echoed" once at the cave mouth and lost an hour he can't account for, and hides it (~55).
- *avoid:* bristles and deflects when his recklessness near the cave is questioned — "I'm careful, I have instruments."

**Personal thread:** learns the old ways and the new can *sing together* — his wind-catcher becomes part of the fix, not a replacement; the old-vs-new tension resolves warmly.

**Cave stance:** wants to *measure* it and keeps getting too close; intrigue and hubris in one.

### Anske — Visiting wind-ship trader  *(NEW, semi-outsider — npc.anske_trader)*

**Voice:** shrewd, clipped, dry; sailor's idioms; real kindness under the bargaining.

**Backstory & lore:** Trades island to island by wind-ship, grounded at Windroot's dock because the wind's gone too thin to lift her sails. She has seen a breathing-cave island *before* — one that didn't restore in time; its people slowly "went quiet," wandered into their own echoes, and it became a place ships no longer dock. She carries that as a warning she's loath to speak aloud.

**Relationships:** lodges at Tamsin's hearth and trades her news for bread; haggles with Jorin over parts; eyes Coll's wind-catcher as something she might buy or carry word of; keeps a careful distance from the cave; gives the player a clear outsider's read on the village's danger.

**What she knows (stakes & wider world):**
- *facts:* the shape of the wider archipelago and its trade-winds; other islands have breathing caves too; the wind here is thinner than the villagers admit and her ship can't leave until it lifts.
- *beliefs:* you fix the wind *now* and feel about it later; sentiment is ballast in a thin wind.
- *rumors:* dock-talk from a dozen islands, some tall, some not.
- *secrets (gated):* the full fate of the island that didn't recover — she resists telling it, and it's the bible's sharpest scare (~70); a soft spot she hides, that she's stayed longer than trade alone explains (~55).
- *avoid:* steers away from the lost island with brisk trade-talk whenever it comes up below trust.

**Personal thread:** the warning made flesh; if the player succeeds she stays a little past her business — the cozy place earns an outsider's softness, and her cautionary tale becomes the proof of what was saved.

**Cave stance:** wary and knowing; she's the one voice that takes the danger entirely seriously.

## 14. Environmental storytelling ideas

- The hamlet visibly "leans" away from true north — grass streaks, banners, smoke — until the relay is restored, then everything snaps true and the light lifts.
- Gull cliff: full nests, fed chicks, scattered feathers, **no adult gull SFX** — just wind. The absence does the work; birdsong only returns after the player acts on Sela's observation.
- Shrine + pillar share spiral/blue-lichen carving; Jorin's bench + west relay share copper/coil — two visual "systems" the player learns to read.
- Cracked seed-batteries leak amber light that brightens on approach — diegetic "scan me."
- The cave mouth: drifting breath-motes thick on wrong-wind days, carrying faint snatches of old sound (a gull, a half-word, the true note). They thin visibly once the relay is restored.
- The bakehouse: wind-bread on the sill rises tall on good days, sits flat now — a cozy, readable barometer of the island's recovery.
- Warm amber light at homes/hearth/shrine; cooler blue flicker at broken signal-tech (pillar, relay, cave). Ambient fill sits low until restoration, then lifts — the island literally brightens on success.
